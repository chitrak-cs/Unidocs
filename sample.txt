MiniPad sample file
===================
Try the commands on this file:
	- Ctrl-F and search for "dog", then press the arrow keys to jump between matches
	- Ctrl-R to replace every "dog" with "dog"
	- Ctrl-B, move the cursor, then Ctrl-X / Ctrl-V to move text around
	- Ctrl-Z / Ctrl-Y to undo and redo


Welcome to unidocs.
This is a sample nodepad opened in cli mode like nano but with simplified controls.
